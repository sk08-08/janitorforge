JANITOR FORGE

STATUS Beta

Managing bot requests through DMs or other websites is messy. Messages get lost, inappropriate requests slip through, and there's no real way to organize everything. Janitor Forge fixes that — it's a free platform built for Janitor AI creators to handle requests, moderate content, and showcase their work, all in one place.

This bot is your guide to Janitor Forge. Whether you're a creator who needs better tools or a user looking for a smoother experience, this page has everything you need to know.

✦ WHAT CAN IT DO?

Request Forms
Build custom request forms with your own fields — character details, scenario preferences, tags, whatever you need. Each form gets a shareable link you can put in your bot descriptions. No more copy-pasting guidelines in DMs.

Content Filter & Moderation
Every incoming request passes through an extensive banned words filter before it even reaches you. If something gets flagged, it's blocked instantly. You can also create your own custom blocklists, adjust filter sensitivity, and manage everything from a dedicated moderation panel with IP-level blocking for repeat offenders.

Creator Pages (W.I.P)
Build a fully customizable public page to showcase your bots. Add hero sections, image galleries, embedded videos, social links, text blocks, banners, and more — all with a drag & drop editor. Choose your layout, accent colors, fonts, and background style. Share one link instead of a messy bot list.

Worlds & Lorebooks (W.I.P)
Organize your bots into interconnected worlds with lore, characters, and settings. Lorebooks let you manage the backstory and worldbuilding details that tie your creations together. Browse and discover other creators' lorebooks through Atlas.

✦ MORE FEATURES

→ Dashboard — See your stats at a glance: total bots, pending requests, completed requests, active forms, and recent activity.

→ Bot Management — Register and manage all your characters from one place. Tag them, track their status, organize them however you want. Includes a real-time token counter to keep your definitions in check.

→ Kanban Boards — Visual drag-and-drop boards for managing incoming requests. Move submissions between columns (New → In Progress → Done), add notes, and filter by form.

→ Profile Customization — Your public profile page with bio, social links, avatar, banner, featured bots, and privacy settings. Control what others can see.

→ Atlas (W.I.P) — A discovery system where you can save lorebooks, create Worlds, browse bot series, and explore what other creators have built.

→ Collaboration — Work together with other creators on shared characters. Full workspace with inline editing, pull requests, roles, permissions, and a live activity feed.

→ Export & Backup — Export your form submissions as CSV or JSON whenever you need.

→ Resources — A curated directory of Janitor-related references, guides, and useful links, organized by category. Everything you need in one place, managed by the platform.

→ Logs — A transparent feed of platform updates, known issues, and status reports. If something breaks or goes quiet, you'll find it here.

→ Settings — Customize your experience: light/dark theme, notification preferences, data management, keyboard shortcuts, and security (PIN change, session info, account deletion).
